# Content Guidelines — Copywriting Voice

The SpaceProbe digital platform must communicate with scientific authority, technical precision, and absolute clarity. The copywriting style mirrors leading research organizations (NASA JPL, ESA) rather than typical SaaS marketing startups.

---

## 1. Tone & Style Rules

- **Authoritative & Scientific**: Present data objectively. Avoid tech buzzwords (e.g. "disruptive", "AI-powered magic"). Use descriptive technical phrasing (e.g. "machine-learning models", "real-time satellite telemetry mapping").
- **Clear & Minimal**: Keep sentences direct. Break complex concepts into readable, scannable sub-headings.
- **Accurate Telemetry Labels**: Use standardized technical units. Never abbreviate coordinates or storm units incorrectly (e.g. use `nT` for magnetic field intensity, `p/cm³` for plasma density, and `Kp` for planetary index).

---

## 2. Heading Hierarchy & Structural Lengths

- **Single Primary Objective**: Each page or component must serve one primary functional purpose.
- **Paragraph Threshold**: Clamped to a maximum of 4 sentences or `80 words` to preserve high scannability.
- **CTA Philosophy**: Keep buttons highly action-oriented and technical (e.g. "Request Sensor Access", "Export Telemetry Logs" instead of "Get Started" or "Join Now").
- **Heading Order**: Always follow a strict semantic nested hierarchy (`H1` -> `H2` -> `H3` -> `H4`).
