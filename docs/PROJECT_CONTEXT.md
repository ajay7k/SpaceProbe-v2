# Project Context — SpaceProbe Website v2

## Context & Vision

SpaceProbe Pvt. Ltd. is an enterprise focused on space science, geomagnetic storm monitoring, geospatial analytics, and education outreach. 

Website v2 is a complete rewrite from scratch (not a refactor of v1). The goal is to unify SpaceProbe's corporate presence and real-time Space Weather forecasting platform into one cohesive digital ecosystem.

## Target User Profiles

1. **Space Weather Researchers & Analysts**: Depend on real-time data feeds, geomagnetic indices (Kp), and historic solar storm logs to model space weather patterns.
2. **Satellite & Grid Operators**: Require instant alerts (NOAA scale events) to mitigate damage to satellites, communications systems, and power grids.
3. **Geospatial Analytics B2B Clients**: Search for geospatial mapping capabilities, case studies, and corporate contact paths.
4. **Academic Community & Students**: Access research posters, Semantic Scholar publications feeds, and educational guides.

## Reference Materials

The original v1 codebases (`spaceprobe` corporate website and the sandboxed `space-weather-frontend` React SPA) serve as a baseline for:
- Form validation limits.
- Core NOAA solar wind endpoints and Kp color scale requirements.
- Publication databases schemas.
- Research posters metadata assets.
